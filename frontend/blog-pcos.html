What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormone‑related condition that can cause irregular periods, extra hair growth, acne, and changes in weight. It is common and many people have it without realising, which is why simple awareness and early checking matter.

PCOS is linked with hormones such as insulin and androgens being out of balance. Because of this, some people gain weight easily or feel tired and low in mood. PCOS is not your fault and it presents differently for everyone.

With support, PCOS can be managed. Doctors may suggest lifestyle changes, medicines, or both to help regulate periods, reduce symptoms, and protect long‑term health. Tracking your cycle and talking openly with a healthcare professional are good first steps.
